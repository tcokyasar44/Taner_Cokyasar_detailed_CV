\makerubrichead{Research Interests}
\vspace{2pt}
{\setlength{\parindent}{0pt}%
My research develops large-scale optimization models and algorithms that make transportation and
logistics systems more efficient, affordable, and resilient. I combine mathematical programming,
decomposition and high-performance computing with agent-based simulation (POLARIS) to answer
planning questions at metropolitan and national scale: where to place EV and electric-bus chargers,
how to schedule transit and delivery fleets and transition them to new vehicle technologies, how
drones and trucks should share last-mile delivery, and how shared and autonomous mobility and
freight supplier choices reshape travel, energy use, and cost.\par}
